Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Top Tag ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you visit our website or use our game.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect or receive:

  • Information you voluntarily provide, such as a username or email address.
  • Game information, such as player IDs, room information and gameplay activity.
  • Technical information, such as device type, browser type and basic usage data.
  • Bug reports and support messages you submit.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain Top Tag.
  • Provide multiplayer and game features.
  • Respond to support requests and bug reports.
  • Improve performance and fix problems.
  • Protect players and help prevent abuse.

3. Sharing Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers that help operate the website or game, when required to provide a feature, or when legally required.

4. Cookies and Third-Party Services

Our website or its hosting and embedded services may use cookies or similar technologies. Third-party services, including hosting, multiplayer and analytics providers, may process information under their own privacy policies.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

6. Data Retention

Information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer period is required by law.

7. Children's Privacy

Top Tag is not intended to collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact us so we can review the request.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information. You can contact us to make a privacy request.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website or game changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.

10. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Top Tag team through our official Discord server.
